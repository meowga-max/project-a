Habitat:

An abandoned traditional Chinese breakfast spot in a parallel universe, inside a forgotten bamboo steamer.

Appearance:

Soft, fluffy and doughy, with pleats that swirl up to a little top knot, two stubby dough arms, small dark eyes and pink cheeks.

Key Characteristics:

  • Feeds on steam.
  • Breathes slowly and gently sways its little arms on the rim of its steamer.
  • When it is heated, it steams up, turns warm pink and stays hot.
  • Each one is born a little different: number of pleats, eye spacing, arm length and dough colour.

What we know so far about [ XL ]? Steamer Spirit has haunted an abandoned breakfast spot since it was first spotted in 1997. It lives inside a forgotten bamboo steamer and survives on steam. When the kitchen is cold, it sits quietly, breathing. Once it is steamed, it warms up and stays hot.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Click on Steamer Spirit to steam it up. A ring of steam bursts out, its dough turns warm pink, its cheeks blush and a big plume of steam rises. After that, it stays hot.
